Brand — Splash & Icon

The two surfaces seen before the ledger: the cold open, and the icon on a home screen. Both carry the identity with the same restraint as the app — paper, charcoal, one seal of vermillion. Nothing here animates, nothing here loads.

Capacitor note The splash is one static frozen frame — a single image per scale, no spinner, no progress bar, no animation. It holds from launch until the first paint of Home, then cross-fades out over motion.normal (240ms). Anything that appears to load would be lying: the ledger is local.
  1. 1余韻 — Noto Serif JP, the only place the serif runs at display size. Letter-spaced so the two characters read as a mark, not as text.
  2. 2“Yoin” — Inter, caption size, wide tracking. It names the mark for anyone who cannot read it, and stays quieter than the mark it names.
  3. 3The whisper line sits in content-muted at the foot, where a colophon would. It earns its place by being the product’s one sentence; it never becomes a tagline anywhere else in the app.
