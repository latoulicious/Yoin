Home History + Insights Settings

The page takes the indigo. Washi cream and sumi trade places — cream becomes the ink, the page becomes the cover material. Nothing else moves: same columns, same 54 / fluid / 124 grid, same rhythm, same copy.

The binding stays darker than the page. In daylight the column bar and the nav are indigo against cream; at night they go darker still (#0A1826) against the indigo page. The relationship is preserved rather than flipped, which is why this reads as the same book and not as a negative of it.

Hairlines subdue, they do not brighten. Rules move to #26405A — visible enough to hold the columns, quiet enough to stay out of the way in a dark room.

Seal vermillion lifts #C3432B → #E05138 to hold its weight against indigo. It is still the only accent, still only on the + and live state.

Brand — Splash & Icon

The book, closed. Both compositions are the same three marks: indigo cover, washi title slip, one vermillion stamp. No interactive elements here, so no annotation dots — notes instead.

Yoin The trace that remains

The cover, not a logo screen. Indigo fills the frame, a blind rule sits 26px in like a stamped border, and the washi title slip is pasted at the left third the way a merchant book is labelled on its board. The vermillion stamp lands below it.

Status bar hidden. A real cold open shows no clock and no battery — only the home indicator survives, drawn by the system. The frame is otherwise the whole screen.

No spinner, no progress, no motion. Capacitor's splash is one static frozen frame: it cannot animate, and it should not pretend to. It holds for as long as the ledger takes to open, then the page replaces it.

余韻 at 34px is the only place the brand is set large. Everywhere else it is a 19px spine mark.

One geometry, two variants. A keeps 余韻 on the slip. B drops the characters below 120px and keeps only the three shapes: indigo field, offset cream bar, vermillion square. Both are the same artwork; B is A with the type turned off.

Ship B. At 60px the characters in A are roughly 8px tall — they read as smudge, not as text, and dirty the one clean edge the icon has. Nothing is lost: at that size the mark is recognised by the offset bar and the red square, never by the reading. Keep A for the 512 store listing and anywhere above 120px.

The circle mask costs the stamp. Android crops the corners, so the stamp sits at 20% / 18% inset rather than tight to the edge — safe under both masks. The iOS squircle here is a 22.5% border-radius approximation, not the true superellipse.

No glyph, no monogram, no pictogram of money. The icon is a book cover seen from above, which is what the app is.
